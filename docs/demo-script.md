# ScanCrowd Demo — 5-Step Demo Script

**Duration:** 5–7 minutes  
**Setup:** Fresh install or reset (long-press logo → confirm)

---

## Step 1 — Welcome & permissions (30 s)

Open the app. The welcome screen explains the concept.  
Tap **Get started** and grant all three permissions (location, camera, notifications).

**Talking points:**
- "Citizens report road defects directly from their phone."
- "AI classifies the defect instantly — no form filling."
- "The city gets structured, geotagged data in real time."

---

## Step 2 — Explore the map (1 min)

The map shows nearby open issues with coloured pins.

- Zoom in on a cluster of pins.
- Tap an issue pin to open the **Issue Tracking** screen.
- Point out the progress bar (Reported → Accepted → Planned → In repair → Fixed).
- Show the timeline: city staff accepted it, assigned a repair week.

**Talking points:**
- "Every issue has a full audit trail — who reported it, when it was accepted, when it was fixed."
- "Citizens get push notifications at each stage without needing an account."

---

## Step 3 — File a report (2 min)

Tap the **camera button** (centre of the tab bar).

1. The camera opens. Point at any object — a kerb, a sign, anything.
2. The AI labels it within 1 second: *"Traffic sign · bent · 94%"*
3. Tap the shutter button. Review the photo on the next screen.
4. Optionally add a note. Tap **Send report**.

The **Sent** screen confirms: *+10 points earned.*

**Talking points:**
- "The whole flow is under 30 seconds."
- "The AI label is a suggestion — the reporter can override it."
- "Faces and licence plates are blurred before the image leaves the device."

---

## Step 4 — Watch the notifications (1–2 min)

After sending, wait 20 seconds (Fast demo speed).

- First notification: *"Report accepted · +10 pts"*
- Second (40 s): *"Fix planned for week N"*
- Third (60 s): *"Work started"*
- Fourth (90 s): *"Issue fixed! +10 bonus points"*

**Talking points:**
- "In production the city triggers these updates from their work management system."
- "Notification latency in the demo is compressed — real timelines are days or weeks."

---

## Step 5 — Leaderboard (1 min)

Tap **Ranks** in the tab bar.

- Show the city leaderboard (Week / Month / Season).
- The user's pinned row shows current rank and points-to-top-3.
- "Top 3 win city service vouchers" — incentive explained.

**Talking points:**
- "Gamification drives repeat reporting. Cities that run seasonal competitions see 3–5× more submissions."
- "The leaderboard is city-scoped — keeps the competition local and meaningful."

---

## After the demo

To reset: long-press the **ScanCrowd** logo for 2 seconds, confirm.  
Or reinstall the APK for a completely clean state.
